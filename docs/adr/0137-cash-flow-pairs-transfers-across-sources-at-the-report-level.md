# Cash flow pairs transfers across sources at the report level

Status: accepted

The Cash flow page shows a move between two of a person's accounts, such as Cathay to Fubon or a Fubon payment of an E.SUN card bill, as one internal transfer. The two sides usually come from different source connections. A Transaction Relation cannot join them: it stays inside one source connection, it comes only from source evidence, and a person cannot create one. The first version also does no cross-source reconciliation.

**A Cash-flow transfer link joins the two sides only at the report level.** It follows the Spending deduplication link. It is revocable and traceable, and it never merges identities or rewrites a source fact. Reliable evidence establishes it automatically: both sides carry evidence-backed `transfer.internal` or `payment.credit_card` Kinds, with equal amount and currency, on close dates. Otherwise a link is a candidate until the person confirms it. A candidate needs equal amount and currency, opposite directions, and two owned non-preview accounts, at most 3 days apart. The person may also pair two such transactions that are further apart themselves.

**A confirmed link decides inclusion only where no evidence does.** A transfer with an opaque bank note gets the fallback Kinds: `purchase` on the outflow and `receipt` on the inflow. Both sides would then count, and inflow and outflow would each grow by the transfer amount. A confirmed or manual link takes a side out of inflow and outflow when that side has a weak or fallback Kind and its counterpart has a weak or fallback Kind or an evidence-backed `transfer.internal`. A `transfer.internal` side is already out and stays out: it proves the move for its counterpart, so an own-account move whose other side reads 跨行轉入 is not counted once as 其他轉入 and again as a pair. Any other evidence-backed Kind, such as `payment.*`, `income.*` or an evidenced `purchase`, decides its own side and keeps the link from excluding the other. A link that leaves a side counted changes nothing, so the transfer note does not count it as a pair, and an internal-transfer side in it shows as unpaired; the note never repeats money a total holds.

**Keyword-based transfer Kinds are weak evidence.** The bank rule assigns `transfer.external` from keywords such as 跨行, 匯款, 電匯 and 轉出至, and those words also describe a move between two of the person's own accounts at different banks. Such a Kind, or a bare `transfer`, cannot tell the two apart. So Cash flow counts it, as 轉帳支出 or 其他轉入, because rent paid by transfer or money sent to family is real flow. A confirmed or manual link may exclude it the same way it excludes a `purchase` or `receipt` fallback, when its counterpart is weak, fallback or `transfer.internal`. It never links by evidence, and `transfer.internal`, `payment.*` and the other evidence-backed Kinds stay as they are.

**Close dates are at most 3 calendar days apart, the same window as a candidate.** A transfer made on a Friday settles by Monday. Evidence links a pair only when it is each side's only candidate and the person has never decided on it; when one side has several candidates, all of them are shown and none is linked. A card bill payment links by evidence when the issuer labels the card's payment credit, such as 網路繳款 or 自動扣繳, and so carries `payment.credit_card` like the bank debit. Both sides are already out as 信用卡繳款, so the link only joins them; the transfer note does not count it, and it excludes nothing else. A card inflow whose description names nothing keeps the direction fallback `refund`, so its bill payment is only ever a candidate.

Decisions are stored like Spending deduplication decisions: an append-only history of confirm, deny, and revoke, and a table of current links. A link from evidence is derived when Cash flow is read and is never stored; revoking one records the revocation, and the pair is then only a candidate. A denied pair is never proposed again and cannot be paired by hand. A stored link applies only while both sides are counted and still meet the candidate rules apart from the date window; otherwise it is suspended. A suspended link changes no inclusion, blocks neither side from candidates or decisions, and applies again once its sides are valid. The person can always revoke it, and a link the person confirms or pairs on one of its sides replaces it and records its revocation. A link from evidence does not replace it: once a suspended link is valid again, it takes precedence over a link from evidence on the same side. Manual Account transactions are never a side: a Manual Account transfer already records both sides with the Kinds the person entered.

## Considered options

- **Pair automatically on amount and time, as the draft shows.** Rejected. Similarity alone would then establish a financial association, which the Spending deduplication link already refuses.
- **Make the pairing a Transaction Relation.** Rejected. It would break the one-source-connection rule and the rule that a person cannot assert financial relations.
- **Do not pair; let Kinds exclude transfers.** Rejected. Opaque transfers would inflate both inflow and outflow, and the page could not show both sides of a transfer.
- **Let a link never affect inclusion.** Rejected for the same inflation. Financial report inclusion already lists relations as an input, and a confirmed Spending deduplication link already changes a total.
- **Let any confirmed link exclude both sides.** Rejected. A person's confirmation would override evidence-backed Kinds.

## Consequences

- A wrong confirmation can remove a real purchase and a real receipt from the totals. The person can revoke it, and the transfer panel lists every confirmed link.
