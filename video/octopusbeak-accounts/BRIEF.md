## Intent

User request (verbatim): 「以下面這個視角產生一段 30秒的 motion graphic video 用於社群媒體的推廣，發揮你作為 motion graphic designer 的創意。」
Angle (verbatim): 多帳戶盤點：「薪轉一間、繳卡費一間、買股票又一間。你要確認自己有多少錢，得打開幾個App？」

Chosen concept (autonomous pitch gate): **「一道算式」** — "how much money do I have?"
is literally a sum whose terms live in different apps. The hook's three phrases each
slam in an app tile (薪轉 富邦, 卡費 玉山, 股票 元大證券); the question makes more tiles
pile up and an "apps opened" counter climb. The tiles then drop into a hand-written
vertical addition (直式加法) on paper where every amount is 「?」. OctopusBeak arrives
and the question marks roll into real numbers like an odometer, a rule line draws, and
the total lands: 淨資產 TWD 2,148,216 — the same number the real 總覽 screen shows.
Left behind: the typical phone-juggling montage of app screens.

Numbers come from the project's real app screenshots: 資產 TWD 3,479,538 (證券 1,284,600 ·
加密資產 809,297 · 銀行 699,160 · 基金 356,200 · 外幣 330,281), 負債 TWD 1,331,322,
淨資產 TWD 2,148,216.
