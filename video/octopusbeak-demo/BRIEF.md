## Intent

A 30-second, silent motion graphic that replaces the demo video in the
landing page's 觀看示範 tile. The user asked for it to show off great motion
design and creativity. Concept (chosen from five pitches, "1 + 2 混合"): one
continuous camera take that follows a single charge, TWD 1,444, from a credit
card statement line → paired with its e-invoice in 帳目合併 → stamped 已合併
→ folded into the month's spending total TWD 13,385 → pulled back to the
financial overview. The site's banknote-engraving language (guilloche rosette,
wave lines, microline rule, engraved Sun Yat-sen) carries the transitions and
acts as the merge "seal".

## Assets

- Pencil reference "zh-TW · Spending v2（帳目合併改為 Modal）" — states A 預設, B 帳目合併 Modal, C 消費明細 Modal（待合併）, D 消費明細 Modal（已合併）, E 無資料; the merge flow is the hero material.
- site/assets/screens/*.webp — real app screenshots (overview, assets, spending, liabilities, sync-data, credentials…).
- site/assets/illustrations/*.webp — engraved Sun Yat-sen poses and the Formosan salmon shoal.
- site/index.html banknote patterns (rosette, waves, microline) and site.css brand tokens.

## Customizations

- No capture of a live site; the brand and assets come from the local site and the Pencil reference.

## Notes

- Plays muted, looped, inside a tile about 350–440px wide: large type, tight framing on amounts and buttons, no reliance on small UI text.
- Music bed added at the user's request (2026-10-06): calm, confident minimal tech underscore with a light pulse; no narration, no SFX. The site tile still autoplays muted, so the music is heard when a viewer unmutes or opens the controls.
- The final MP4 and poster replace site/assets/octopusbeak-demo.mp4 and site/assets/octopusbeak-demo-poster.webp.
