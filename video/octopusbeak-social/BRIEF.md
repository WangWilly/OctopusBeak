## Intent

User request (verbatim): 「使用專案裡面的素材，幫我產生一個30秒的 motion graphic video 用於社群媒體的推廣，內容是繁體中文，幫我介紹這個 app 的運作。發揮你作為 motion graphic designer 的創意。」

Sell, not tour: a promo that explains how the app works. Creative direction was
delegated. Chosen concept (autonomous pitch gate): **「一本存摺」** — the film is a
Taiwanese bank passbook. The deep-blue engraved cover (site banknote language:
guilloche rosette, wave lines, microline) opens on the hook; each step of how the
app works is one passbook page, printed line by line like a passbook printer and
closed with a teal rosette page stamp ①②③: 設定登入資料 → 一鍵同步、自動收集 →
資產、負債、消費一頁看清. The last page is the privacy promise and the brand.
Left behind: the typical UI screen tour with floating device mockups.
